The featured demonstration

The 88-second Piano Simulation Project Demo v6 was prepared on October 8, 2026 and approved for this portfolio. The portfolio copy preserves the demonstration’s pictures, cover and captions at 720p. Its original Unravel soundtrack has been replaced with the project’s Pas de deux recording for this website. The soundtrack illustrates the portfolio and does not follow the notes depicted in the Unravel visuals.

The before / after material

The pictured Unravel project credits the composition to TK / Toru Kitajima and the piano arrangement to Animenz. This website includes no Unravel soundtrack, raw score or reference recording.

Comparison images are extracted from frozen Unravel full-performance v1 and v2, both dated September 29, 2026, at matching master times 00:47 and 03:55. The v2 delivery records hand-surface, gaze, character and gesture revisions, with exact reuse of v1’s encoded audio. Still images show particular moments; they are not a continuous-motion evaluation.

Sound and authorship

The optional background track is the project’s locally rendered Tchaikovsky Pas de deux piano performance, extracted from master seconds 10–58 and gently faded at the excerpt boundaries. It plays only after an explicit choice. The demo uses master seconds 10–98 from that same recording, with entrance and exit fades. No reference artist’s recording is included.

The case study describes human direction of AI-assisted engineering: creative requirements, workflow design, versioned review and targeted revisions.
