What the project
builds from.

Piano Performance Simulation combines musical interpretation, synthesized sound, character animation and a separate note layer. The examples retain their own sources and development history.

Score and performance interpretation

A score supplies pitches and musical structure. Timing, dynamics, fingering and pedal are interpreted for the performance. The falling bars use physical note-contact events; audible decay can continue after a key is released.

The contact chart samples one second of the current Winter Wind finger-contact events. The moving diagnostic excerpt and finger/rig stills come from the earlier Unravel implementation shown in the completed walkthrough.

Sound and animated imagery

The featured piano soundtrack was synthesized locally from the interpreted note timeline. It was not copied from a reference pianist’s recording. Every viewing format uses the published softer-phrase soundtrack.

The character, instrument, room and cameras come from the software-rendered scene. Falling notes are composited as a separate layer. The hero and the pianist-with-visualizer format share the same approved performance video and soundtrack.
